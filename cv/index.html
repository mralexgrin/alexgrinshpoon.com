New York area · alexgrinshpoon.com · linkedin.com/in/alexgrinshpoon · gsasha21@gmail.com

Summary

I design products and lead the teams that build them. For about twenty years I've worked on direct-to-consumer, eCommerce and publishing products at Barnes & Noble, Newell Brands, Pearson and now The Arena Group, home of Sports Illustrated and TheStreet. I set the direction, hire and grow designers, and still draw the flows and screens myself.

What I do best

  • Hands-on product design. User flows, wireframes, prototypes and production UI. I still work in the files.
  • Getting from a vague brief to a decision. I write the brief, explore options side by side, and walk executives through the tradeoffs until we pick one.
  • Commerce and monetization. Checkout, subscriptions, metered paywalls, in-article purchase and ad-supported templates.
  • Design systems that hold up across many brands and many teams.
  • Research and testing. I set up user research and A/B testing programs and feed what we learn back into the roadmap.
  • Building teams. I hire, mentor and run multidisciplinary design teams of 8 to 10+ people.
