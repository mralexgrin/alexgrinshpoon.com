Pearson.com

A new front door for Pearson.com: a personalized store for students and professionals advancing their careers.

Company
Pearson
Year
2020
Role
Product design, product strategy, taxonomy and content design
Project
Reimagine

The brief

In early 2020, Pearson.com needed a new front door for students and professionals. The project, Reimagine, spanned eCommerce, a video learning app, and a new design language.

A personal front door

A home page that greets the learner by name and picks up where they left off.

Reimagined eCommerce flow

A new store flow from category to checkout, built to serve college students and working professionals.

A video-based learning app

A dashboard and player for video courses: progress, live training, and upcoming exams.

The design language

A design language that broke from the traditional Pearson look and became the foundation for future products.
